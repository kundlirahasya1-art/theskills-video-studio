# "Hyper-realistic" bina credit ke: sach kya hai

Ye repo asli AI-generated footage **nahi** banata. Free raaste:

1. **Asli footage / apni shoot:** sabse realistic, free. Phone se bhi chalega.
2. **Free stock clips:** Pexels, Pixabay (commercial use ki license padh lo). Unhe `reel` me jodo.
3. **AI clips sirf zaroorat par:** Google Flow / Veo me kam clips banao, baaki sab yahan (kaatna, caption, music, shorts) free me.
4. **Local AI video models:** free hain par badi GPU chahiye. Mac Air par practical nahi.

Isliye credit bachane ka tareeka: AI se kam, lambi video ko kaatkar bahut saare shorts yahan banao.
Kisi asli insaan ki shakal/awaaz bina uski ijazat ke mat banao.
